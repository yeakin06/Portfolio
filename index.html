Intro

Hi, I’m Hossain Ahmed Yeakin (@Yeakin06).

I’m a self-driven developer and lifelong learner who loves turning ideas into meaningful digital experiences. From building websites and bots to experimenting with new technologies, I’m always exploring, creating, and learning something new.
